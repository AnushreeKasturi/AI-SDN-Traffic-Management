The interesting finding: more learning didn’t mean better routing.

A class-aware Dijkstra profile chosen on validation data performed best overall. The Q-learning hybrids are useful ablations, but did not beat the fixed profile on mean or voice/video latency. That result also held on the second, unseen network.

Inspect the complete campus topology

8 OpenFlow switches, 6 hosts, and 18 links. Redundant paths let the adaptive controller route around busy links. Core capacities range from 60 to 100 Mbps; each host has a 1,000 Mbps access link.

Intelligence, with the evidence.

Two Random Forest models handle different jobs: understand each flow, then anticipate what the links will do next.
